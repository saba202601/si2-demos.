    FOUNDATION · CANONICAL MODEL

    P&C canonical data model and master

    One governed vocabulary for policy, party, location, risk, coverage, premium, claim and bureau data. Find any element by name or alias and see its pick list, values, codes and every form and system that uses it.

    NEW · ROLE DESKS

    Role-based super intelligence

    A desk for every role, from executive to adjuster: referrals, alerts, authority and rules in plain English, with impact checked before a change and approval by a second person.

    NEW · DATA BRIDGE

    Any source to any destination

    Legacy extracts to a modern policy system and the bureaus through the canonical model: value translation, held records with reasons, and lineage for every value.

    NEW · CLAIMS LEAKAGE

    Claim coverage check

    Reads the policy forms (covered, not covered, exclusions) and checks each claim: the adjuster is alerted on coverage, the underwriter and claims manager on limit or deductible changes, SIU on split payments.

    BUSINESS RULES

    P&C business rule engine

    Eligibility, referral, pricing, compliance, billing and claims rules by company, line, product and state, with generated tests and an audit trail for every decision.

    BUREAU REPORTING

    Bureau reporting workbench

    Read the WCIO specs, map your source automatically, generate WCSTAT, WCPOLS, WCIND and WCMED files, and turn bureau errors into approved fixes.

    BUREAU REPORTING · ISO

    ISO statistical reporting, all lines

    Personal and commercial lines on effective-dated ISO layouts: source to canonical to the 150-byte record, with every field, code and rule approved by your SME before it is filed.

    RATING

    Rating engine

    CLAIMS

    LEGACY TO CLOUD MIGRATION

    FRAUD
