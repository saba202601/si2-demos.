How it is generated

One store, two projections

The CDM and the ontology are not built by two teams. They are two outputs of one table set: objects, elements, scopes, pick lists, rules and rating steps. Change a row once and both outputs change together.

The context key

Why the keys matter

A field is not simply "required". It is required for a country, a state, a writing company, a line, a product edition, an object and a module, from a date. The context key records exactly that. A star (*) means "all", and the most specific matching row wins.

KeyMeaningExamplesWhy it matters
country

By country

  • United States: states, ZIP codes, FEIN, private workers' comp in most states.
  • Canada: provinces, postal codes, business number instead of FEIN; workers' comp is provided by provincial boards, so the WC class is not applicable.
  • Great Britain: no states, postcodes, and employers' liability is compulsory.
state

By state: property and auto

  • Florida, Texas: named-storm or windstorm percentage deductible required; Florida also needs the flood zone.
  • California: earthquake percentage deductible.
  • Florida, New York, Michigan, New Jersey: no-fault personal injury protection on commercial auto.
state

By state: workers' comp and liability

  • Ohio, Washington, North Dakota, Wyoming: monopolistic state funds, so no private class payroll (stop-gap employers' liability only).
  • California, New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania: independent bureaus instead of NCCI.
  • New York: contractors must answer the Labor Law 240/241 exposure question.
company

By writing company

  • Admitted paper: filed rates and forms; no surplus lines tax.
  • Excess and surplus paper: surplus lines tax always, stamping fee in states with a stamping office (for example Texas, New York, California).
  • Each company in a group can carry its own pick-list options and rules.
lob · product

By line and product edition

  • General liability claims-made product needs a retroactive date; occurrence forms do not.
  • Commercial property uses construction class, protection class and roof year; the other lines do not.
  • Old policies keep the product edition they were written on.
object · module · dates

By object, module and date

  • Object: an element belongs to one object (Building, Vehicle, Driver, Class) wherever it is used.
  • Module: claims needs the injured worker's average weekly wage; finance needs the annual statement line; claims does not need the FEIN.
  • Dates: nothing is deleted; a change ends one row and starts the next.

Most specific wins

For a Florida commercial property policy, the row state=FL, lob=CP beats the row lob=CP, which beats the base definition. Specificity counts the keys that are not *. The same rule runs in SQL (cdm.fn_ResolveElement), in the ontology (each applicability is an individual carrying its key) and in the resolver below.

Keeping it current

When a source system changes

SI² watches every connected source. A new field in Guidewire, a new class code from a bureau, a new ACORD form version or a changed legacy copybook becomes a proposed change to the store, and both outputs follow.

Mandatory alert

Act now

  • Regulatory or bureau changes with an effective date: a new state rule, class code or statistical plan edition.
  • A change that breaks a mapping in production.
  • The owner is alerted and the change is tracked to closure by its due date.
Release approval

Bundle and approve

  • New optional fields, new synonyms, label changes, new pick-list options.
  • Grouped into a release; an SME approves and a second person signs off.
  • The CDM and the ontology are published together with the release number.
